Milled and Powercoated Rubik's Cube

I CAM'ed and machined a rubix cube that my co-worker, the amazing scientist and artist Ruza Markov, designed using pythonocc. I designed and milled stencils for each face of the cube and powder coated each face using a powdercoating gun and powders from Eastwood, an old microwave, and a aluminum holder I designed and milled to keep the parts together.
