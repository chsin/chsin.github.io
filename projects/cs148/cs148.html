Stanford CS148 Computer Graphics and Imaging Project

Inspiration

I made that rough sketch in high school while volunteering at the Brooklyn Botanical Gardens, inspired in part by the BBG's Japanese Garden and the Itsukushima Torii (a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Japan). The boat at the gate was inspired by in part by the Tōrō nagashi floating lanterns festival where the dead are mourned and sent off.

Geometry, Models and Textures

I made all the geometry in the scene except for the gate, which was downloaded from blendswap but I made the gate's texture. I CADed the boats in Solidworks using photos as a references, exported as .stl's and imported into Blender where I further edited the geometry and UV-mapped it for texutring and then exported them as .obj's. I made the textures using Pinta, a Linux paint program. The sky is a plane textured with an image I painted for mountains. The water is a plane textured with a photo of water and a normal map I made using CrazyBump.

Lighting

The scene only contains 16 point lights, corresponding to the 16 boats, all added in with a for-loop with position vectors that I specified. Added attentuation functionality to raytracer.
